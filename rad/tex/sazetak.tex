\chapter*{Sažetak}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sažetak}

Obveznik u Hrvatskoj od 2026.\ mora svaki eRačun dostaviti primatelju i prijaviti
ga Poreznoj upravi, i to dvama kanalima koje propis ne povezuje. Rad izvodi načine
na koje se taj tijek može pokvariti, i to iz Zakona, Pravilnika i tehničke
specifikacije, pa je svaka tvrdnja provjerljiva bez pristupa ijednom sustavu.

Izvedeno je dvanaest načina otkazivanja. Najjači pojedinačni nalaz je da šifra
S008 istodobno znači da je ponovljena prijava prošla i da je propisani ispravak
odbijen. Ispravak se izdaje pod istim brojem računa, a indikator kopije nije dio
identifikatora po kojem se provjerava duplikat, pa provjera koja štiti od
duplikata odbija tijek koji isti propis nalaže.

Iz toga slijedi teza rada. Dio slučajeva nesvodiv je, ali ne zbog svojstava
raspodijeljene razmjene, nego zato što ih propis nalaže ili zato što propis
uklanja mehanizam koji bi ih zatvorio. Pokrivenost je ocijenjena matricom od
dvanaest načina otkazivanja i šest obrazaca, uz označenu razinu dokaza za svaku
ćeliju. Vrijednost \emph{uklanja} ne nastupa nijednom, a sedam ćelija pokazuje
obrazac koji usklađenost pogoršava umjesto da je poboljša. Granica pokrivenosti
time nije tehnička nego pravna, i pomiče se izmjenom propisa, ne boljim dizajnom.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Ključne riječi:} tolerancija na greške, raspodijeljeni sustavi,
fiskalizacija eRačuna, obrasci otpornosti, nesvodivost, referentna arhitektura,
mjerilo izvedeno iz propisa

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

From 2026, a Croatian business subject to fiscalization must deliver every
eInvoice to its recipient and report it to the Tax Administration, through two
channels that the regulation does not connect. This thesis derives the ways in
which that flow can break. The derivation uses the Act, the Ordinance and the
technical specification, so every claim is verifiable without access to any
system.

Twelve failure modes are derived. The strongest single finding is that the code
S008 means both that a repeated report was accepted and that a mandated
correction was rejected. A correction is issued under the same invoice number,
and the copy indicator is not part of the identifier used for the duplicate
check, so the check that protects against duplicates rejects the flow that the
same rules mandate.

From this follows the thesis of the work. Some failure modes are irreducible, but
not because of properties of distributed exchange. They are irreducible because
the regulation mandates them, or because the regulation removes the mechanism
that would close them. Coverage is assessed with a matrix of twelve failure modes
and six patterns, with a labelled evidence level for every cell. The value
\emph{removes} never occurs, and seven cells show a pattern that worsens
compliance instead of improving it. The limit of coverage is therefore legal
rather than technical, and it moves by amending the regulation, not by better
design.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fault tolerance, distributed systems, eInvoice
fiscalization, resilience patterns, irreducibility, reference architecture,
regulation-derived oracle
